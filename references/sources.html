17 Major VPN provider says it could leave Canada over lawful access bill The Canadian Press / BNN Bloomberg — https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/2026/05/15/major-vpn-provider-says-it-could-leave-canada-over-lawful-access-bill/ 2026-05-15 Public Safety spokesperson Simon Lafortune: government rejects the backdoor characterisation and says legal authorization such as a court warrant would still be required

Why this needs care. Source 17 is the government’s position, not an undisputed description of the bill. Sources 6 and 15 record that, as introduced, an officer with reasonable grounds to suspect could demand that a telecommunications service provider confirm whether a person is a subscriber, without prior judicial authorization. Any brochure sentence asserting that a warrant “remains necessary” must be attributed to the government rather than stated as fact.
