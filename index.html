SNARKYPUSS

A private bridge to your VPN.

Snarkypuss is privately hosted, open-source software for building and controlling your own bridge to a commercial VPN service. Your computer connects through a private WireGuard tunnel to a small hosted server you control, and the commercial VPN runs there instead of on your machine.

There is no Snarkypuss company, subscription, or central network carrying anyone's traffic. Each installation belongs to its user.

Install it

Setting up Snarkypuss takes some technical knowledge: a hosted virtual server of your own, and an account with a supported commercial VPN provider. The source, documentation and installation instructions are on GitHub.
